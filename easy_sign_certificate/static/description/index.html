Seal signed PDFs
with your company certificate

When every signer has signed, the final PDF is also signed cryptographically with your company certificate (.p12 / .pfx), so PDF readers show a signature panel and detect any later change.

Odoo 19 · Community & EnterpriseAdd-on for Easy SignNo external service

How it works

Three steps

1

Upload the certificate

Add your .p12 / .pfx file and its password in Settings › Technical › Certificates, with Odoo's standard certificate manager.

2

Select it for Easy Sign

In Easy Sign › Configuration › Settings choose the signing certificate of the company.

3

Completed PDFs are sealed

Each completed request produces a PDF signed with that certificate. Without a certificate the flow stays the same.

Trusted readers. Use a certificate issued by a recognised authority so that Adobe Acrobat Reader and other readers show the signature as trusted. A self-signed certificate still protects the file, but readers mark its identity as unknown.
